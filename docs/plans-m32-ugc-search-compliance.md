## 1. Why this milestone exists

Research question posed: *"Google's rule about real people writing posts, and how it relates
to a social app."* Answer, from Google's primary documentation:

**Google has no real-name or human-authorship requirement. It requires content that is
created for people, authored by a genuine (non-fabricated) source, and not mass-produced to
manipulate rankings.**

| # | Google rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | "Who, how, why" self-assessment: is it self-evident who authored the content, do bylines lead to more information about the author, is automation disclosed when a reader would wonder, and is the *why* people-first? Using automation/AI **for the primary purpose of manipulating rankings** is a spam-policy violation. | [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) (last updated 2026-10-01) |
| R2 | **Scaled content abuse**: "many pages generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings" — explicitly **method-agnostic**: "whether automation, humans, or a combination." Ex: generative-AI pages without added value, scraping, stitching, keyword-mush pages. "Exclude it from Search." | [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies); [Google blog, 2024-03-05](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/google-search-update-march-2024/) |
| R3 | AI/Automation is **not** against guidelines when content is helpful; the production method is not the test — quality and helpfulness are. | Google Search Central, *How Google Search views AI-generated content* (2023), reaffirmed in R1 |
| R4 | **Fake E-E-A-T is low quality**: "AI-generated content with made-up 'author' profiles (AI generated images or deceptive creator descriptions) in order to make it appear that the content is written by people" and false credential claims are explicit Lowest-quality examples. R1 adds: "Fabricating creator profiles … is a form of deception … a signal of a low-quality page." | Search Quality Rater Guidelines §4.5.3 (Jan 2025 revision; Sept 2025 edition) |
| R5 | **You are the publisher of every user post**: "Google doesn't differentiate between content you wrote and content your users wrote. If you publish it, it's the content that you want to have published." Official playbook: publish an abuse policy, let users report spam, **`noindex` posts from users with no reputation** (lift later), mark links in untrusted content `rel="nofollow"`/`ugc`, use manual approval for suspicious submissions, blocklist repeat offenders, block automated signups. | [Prevent user-generated spam](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/prevent-abuse) (updated 2025-12-10); Google Search Central video *User-generated content on your site* |
| R6 | Qualify outbound links: `rel="ugc"` for user-placed links, `rel="sponsored"` for paid, `rel="nofollow"` when you don't endorse. All are **hints**; combinations allowed (`rel="ugc nofollow"`). | [Qualify outbound links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links); [Evolving nofollow, 2019-09-10](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2019/09/evolving-nofollow-new-ways-to-identify) |
| R7 | If monetized (AdSense), the publisher is responsible for **all** UGC on pages carrying ad code; reviews "primarily generated by an automated program or artificial intelligence" must be marked as spam. | [AdSense UGC overview](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1355699); Merchant Center Product Ratings policies |

**Applied to YoTop10**: users are anonymous and some use AI. Neither fact is a violation.
What Google actually needs from a social app is (a) a stable, verifiable "Who", (b) no
fabricated identity, (c) quality/oversight gates on the *content*, and (d) containment of
thin/spammy UGC.

## 2. Decisions (adopted 2026-10-03)

| ID | Decision | Rationale |
|----|----------|-----------|
| **D1** | **Anonymous but accountable.** No real-name requirement. Every indexed post carries a byline → permanent profile (`/a/{slug}`) showing member-since, post history, approval rate, trust tier. | This *is* Google's "Who" (R1). The Reddit/pseudonym model. A legal name adds nothing Google asks for. |
| **D2** | **No fabricated identity.** Never: AI headshots posing as a person, invented credentials, or synthetic/seed authors in production (`any_seed` and friends must stay dev-only). | Explicit Lowest-quality trigger (R4). |
| **D3** | **Judge the post, not the tool.** Never use "AI-written vs human-written" as an indexation, ranking, or moderation signal. It is unverifiable and not Google's test (R3). | Index-gating on provenance would be security theater. |
| **D4** | **Reputation-based indexation** (Google's own prescription, R5): a post is indexable only when its author has **≥1 approved post AND account age ≥ 7 days AND `trust_score` ≥ 1.0**. Below threshold → `noindex, follow`. It lifts automatically when thresholds are met. | Directly implements R5; applies equally to human and AI-assisted posts (D3). |
| **D5** | **Optional AI disclosure, author-toggled**: `Post.ai_assisted: boolean`, default `false`, shown as a voluntary badge + a site-level policy statement in `/docs/guidelines` ("posts may be AI-assisted; moderation may be automated"). **Never** a ranking/indexation signal. | Answers R1's "How" question honestly without demanding identity. |
| **D6** | **Every user-placed outbound link** carries `rel="ugc nofollow noopener noreferrer"`. Internal `next/link` anchors untouched. Trustworthy-contributor exceptions are **out of scope** for M32. | R6; cheapest correct default for a platform where links are user-submitted. |
| **D7** | **Thin profiles are not indexed**: profile pages get `noindex` when bio is empty **and** the author has 0 approved posts; those profiles are excluded from `sitemap-profiles.xml`. | R5 + crawl hygiene. Google's guidance is to index UGC *by default only when you're happy with its quality*. |
| **D8** | **Keep the 14 machine-named profiles** (`a_dbb4_aed5`, …). They are real anonymous device identities, not test data → no purge. D7 contains the thin ones. | Purging would destroy real users' content/identity (D1). |
| **D9** | **Identity URLs must be unique.** `toPublicSlug('a_dbb4_aed5') → 'dbb4'` (4-hex) can collide across anonymous accounts, mapping two people to one profile URL. Must be fixed before M32.3 makes profiles load-bearing authorship signals. | A colliding byline breaks the "Who" signal (R1) and creates duplicate content. |

**Out of scope for M32**: real-name verification, email requirements, AdSense integration,
AI-detection tooling, purging accounts, changing trust-score math, `double_blind` behaviour
(config exists but is not consumed by any route — authors are always rendered).

### Global gates (every task, per AGENTS.md §2.1)
`cd backend && pnpm typecheck && pnpm lint && pnpm test` and
`cd frontend && pnpm typecheck && pnpm lint && pnpm test`, plus a frontend build gate.
Inside the dev container: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `ESLINT_USE_FLAT_CONFIG=false npx eslint …`,
`npx vitest run`.

Two valid build gates (measured 2026-10-03):
- **Fast (~3.5 min)** — scratch-dir build inside `yotop10_dev`, never in `/app/frontend`:
  ```bash
  docker exec yotop10_dev bash -lc 'set -e; rm -rf /tmp/febuild; mkdir -p /tmp/febuild
    cd /app/frontend && tar --exclude=.next --exclude=node_modules -cf - . | tar -xf - -C /tmp/febuild
    ln -s /app/frontend/node_modules /tmp/febuild/node_modules
    cd /tmp/febuild && NODE_ENV=production npx next build'
  ```
- **Definitive (~11.5 min)** — `docker compose -p yotop10 -f docker-compose.yml build frontend`.

**Never** build in place at `/app/frontend` (it fights the running `frontend-dev` pm2 process
